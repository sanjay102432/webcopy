01 / Method

We don’t watch.
We read.

Most motion audits rely on a recording. We prefer the source. webcopy fetches the public document, follows its stylesheets and scripts, and reconstructs what the page intends to do.

That means you see the systems — not just the frames that happened to be on screen. The result is a dense, exportable readout for designers, engineers and the incurably curious.
